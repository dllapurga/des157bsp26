Usability Results

Observations

  • All users were impressed by the "wholesome" aspect of my project and appreciated how refreshing of a theme it was in comparison to the other projects in the class that were about more sobering topics such as post-grad plans.
  • Some users paused after click and rotating on the lazy-susan, almost hesitant to scroll. It was as if they were unsure if they should scroll.
  • Some users were a little thrown off by who the project was targeted for and who the POV of the written text was coming from. I think they needed a little more context to understand what this project's purpose was.

Questions

Points of Improvement

  • Make it clearer who this project is for/directed towards
  • Shorten AOS delays. If it's delayed too much, it may not show up in time and people may assume nothing is there and move on.
  • Adjust the size, color, background or weight of the instruction font so that it's not easily missed
  • Adjust text and paper so that there's a little more negative space around the text on the paper slips

Is It Navigable?

  • Users can generally easily navigative my project as there are only 2 primary action items. One is to click on the lazy-susan to cycle through recipes and the other is to just scroll to view the contents for each recipe. Both of those actions are explicitely communicated to the user, so there's generally not really any confusion on how to interact with it.

Does It Impact The Users The Way I Intended?

  • Absolutely. Despite the few corrections I can make, I observed immediate smiles of the users' faces. They immediately mentioned how wholesome, nostalgic, and refreshing the project felt, and that's what I was hoping to evoke. I designed the visuals to resemble that of a grandmother's kitchen taking inspiration from the tile my grandmother has on her kicthen floors. It wanted it to feel comfortable and like a safe place to return to when you need a reminder of where and who you come from.

Modifications and How I Plan To Update

  • Shorten AOS delay
  • Perhaps add a button at the bottom of each screen bringing the user back up to the top to reclick the lazy-susan
  • Perhaps try to make it so that when clicking the left of the lazy-susan, it rotates counter-clockwise 90 degrees and when clicking the right of the lazy-susan, it rotates clockwise.
  • Make instructions more visiably obvious
  • Change cover screen brief so that the text introducses the target audience
